# Support

CUT-HRMERS is an internal institutional system. The repository does not publish a public end-user support address.

- **Bug or feature request:** open the matching GitHub issue form with synthetic data only. Do not include real employee records, access tokens, or private document links.
- **Production incident or account-access issue:** contact the CUT-HRMERS project owner or the institution's designated Supabase/hosting administrator through the approved internal channel. Include the incident time, route or workflow, affected environment, and redacted error details.
- **Security vulnerability:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue.
- **Database migration or notification failure:** use the [operations runbook](docs/operations.md) and contact the authorized environment owner. Do not manually alter production data to work around a failure.

Maintainers should add the institution-approved support contact and incident escalation path here once the project owner supplies them.
