# CUT Human Resource Management and Employee Retention System

## Agreed direction

The system is a day-to-day, HR-only management system for Chinhoyi University of Technology (CUT). It must be useful every working day, whether or not an employee leaves the University.

Employee retention and exit analysis are integrated capabilities, not the system's only purpose. Asset management and cross-departmental clearance workflows are outside this system's scope.

## Core objective

To develop a centralised HR management system for CUT that supports daily employee administration, HR requests, employee records, contracts, leave, staff engagement, workforce monitoring, and exit management, while providing reliable information for HR decision-making and employee retention.

## Users and access

| User | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| System Administrator | Manages accounts, permissions, system configuration, and technical audit access. Does not make HR decisions or automatically access confidential HR cases. |
| HR Staff | Maintains employee records; processes requests, leave, contracts, onboarding, cases, engagement, retention, and exit workflows; views authorised reports. HR Officer and HR Manager are permission levels within this group. |
| Employees | Academic and non-academic staff. They view their own permitted records, submit and track HR requests and leave, view authorised documents, take part in surveys, submit resignations, and complete relevant forms. |

Academic and non-academic staff remain one Employee user role. Their staff category is stored in their employee profile for administration and reporting.

## Daily HR operations

The HR dashboard is a working control centre. It provides direct links to records requiring action:

- employee records needing verification;
- leave requests and employee requests awaiting action;
- contracts approaching expiry;
- probation, onboarding, and follow-up milestones due;
- open employee-relations cases;
- overdue engagement or retention actions; and
- exit cases awaiting completion.

On quieter days, HR uses the system for staff records, workforce planning, contract monitoring, policy-related administration, reporting, and scheduled staff engagement activities.

## Functional scope

1. **Employee records and staff establishment**: official staff register, academic and non-academic categories, faculties, departments, positions, employment status, appointment dates, grades, employment history, and—if approved by CUT—establishment and vacancy information.
2. **Leave and absence management**: employee leave applications, balances and statuses; HR rules, records, absence patterns, and upcoming leave monitoring.
3. **Contracts, appointments, and probation**: contracts and appointment letters, expiry tracking, probation milestones, and reminders for reviews or confirmations.
4. **Employee self-service and HR requests**: profile correction requests, employment letters, questions, permitted documents, request tracking, assignment, and resolution.
5. **Onboarding and staff development**: appointments, induction, required training, professional development, qualifications, and certifications.
6. **Employee relations and case management**: restricted HR handling of grievances, workplace concerns, disciplinary matters, follow-ups, outcomes, owners, and deadlines.
7. **Engagement and retention**: periodic surveys, employee check-ins, recurring concerns, retention actions, and evaluation of those actions.
8. **Exit management and workforce analytics**: exit reasons and interviews, exit workflows, headcount, vacancies, contract changes, feedback, retention activities, and workforce reporting.

## Delivery priorities

### Stage 1 — Essential HR operations

Authentication and permissions, employee register, departments, contracts, document management, HR dashboard, employee self-service, requests, notifications, and audit trails.

### Stage 2 — Daily workflows

Leave and absence management, onboarding, probation, HR case management, employment letters, contract reminders, and operational reporting.

### Stage 3 — Engagement, retention, and exits

Surveys, check-ins, confidential feedback, retention actions, exit interviews, exit cases, and intervention tracking.

### Stage 4 — Advanced reporting

Turnover rates, staffing changes, workforce trends, two-year comparisons when data exists, exports, and evaluation of HR interventions.

## Boundaries to validate with CUT HR

Before final requirements are approved, confirm:

1. Which existing activities are paper-based, spreadsheet-based, or handled in another HR system.
2. Which actions need an HR Officer, HR Manager, or other authorised approval.
3. What employee, contract, leave, appointment, and historical exit data exists.
4. Whether payroll is outside the system, integrated, or limited to relevant HR information.
5. Who can access confidential cases, surveys, employment documents, and workforce reports.

Payroll calculation, recruitment automation, advanced performance management, and external integrations are not included unless CUT confirms a clear operational need and authority.
